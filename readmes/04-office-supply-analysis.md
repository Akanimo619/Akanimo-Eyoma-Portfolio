# Office Supply Analysis

## Project Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard examining office-supply revenue, cost, product performance, payment channels, monthly trends and customer segments.

## Objective
- Monitor total revenue and cost.
- Identify top products by revenue and units sold.
- Compare payment channels.
- Track monthly revenue.
- Compare average revenue across customer segments.

## Tools
- Tableau
- Calculated fields
- Interactive filters
- KPI design
- Sales analytics

## Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard displays total revenue of $6,983,336 and total cost of $7,277,985.

## Key Observations
- Biro is the leading product by total revenue in the displayed view at about $2.00M.
- Biro also leads the displayed units-sold ranking with 8,344 units.
- Cash accounts for 38% of the payment-channel mix shown.
- October is the peak month in the displayed monthly revenue series.

## Skills Demonstrated
KPI development, product analysis, payment-channel analysis, time-series reporting, filtering and dashboard composition.
