# Food & Beverage Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
A Tableau workbook analyzing food and beverage sales performance across revenue, orders, product categories, product groups, sales channels and salesperson performance.

## Objective
- Monitor total revenue and total orders.
- Compare revenue across sales channels and categories.
- Identify top product groups by revenue.
- Compare salesperson performance by revenue and order volume.
- Analyze revenue over time.

## Tools
- Tableau
- Calculated fields
- Interactive filters
- KPI design
- Sales analytics

## Workbook Analysis
The workbook contains views for distribution of revenue by channel, monthly revenue by order number, top product groups by revenue, top salespeople by revenue, top salespeople by highest orders, total orders by product category, total revenue by category and ATP.

## Skills Demonstrated
Sales analytics, KPI development, calculated fields, ranking analysis, time-series analysis and interactive dashboard design.
