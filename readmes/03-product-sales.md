# Product Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
A Tableau profitability and revenue analysis using KPI cards, time-series views, category analysis and geographic profit analysis.

## Objective
- Monitor revenue, cost and profit.
- Examine revenue trends by year and month.
- Compare profit across product categories.
- Explore profitability by country.

## Tools
- Tableau
- Calculated fields
- KPI design
- Time-series analysis
- Geographic analysis

## Dashboard Highlights
The supplied Tableau dashboard shows revenue of 84,826,772, cost of 52,780,533 and profit of 32,046,239.

## Key Observations
- Profit is displayed as 32,046,239.
- Revenue rises substantially across the later months shown in the dashboard.
- Product-level profit comparisons highlight differences between high- and low-performing products.
- The workbook includes country-level and category-level profitability views.

## Skills Demonstrated
Dashboard design, KPI analysis, calculated fields, time-series visualization, profitability analysis and business storytelling.
