# Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
An interactive Tableau dashboard for e-commerce sales analysis across order status, geography, customer demographics and sales channels.

## Objective
- Track sales and order volume.
- Understand order completion status.
- Compare sales across genders and age groups.
- Identify top-performing states and channels.
- Explore geographic sales distribution.

## Tools
- Tableau
- E-commerce analytics
- Geographic visualization
- Interactive filters

## Dashboard Highlights
The supplied dashboard shows total sales of 21,176,377 and total orders of 31,047.

## Key Observations
- Delivered orders represent 93.08% of the displayed order-status distribution.
- Women account for 13.56M of displayed sales compared with 7.61M for men.
- Maharashtra leads the displayed top-five state ranking at 2.99M.
- Amazon is the leading displayed channel at 7.52M.

## Skills Demonstrated
Customer analytics, channel analysis, geographic visualization, KPI reporting and interactive dashboard design.
