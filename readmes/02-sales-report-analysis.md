# Sales Report Analysis

## Project Overview
A four-page Power BI sales reporting solution covering overall sales performance, branch and zone performance, employee performance and product sales.

## Objective
- Track overall sales KPIs.
- Compare branches and zones.
- Evaluate employee performance.
- Identify high-performing products.
- Allow users to filter the report by branch and zone.

## Tools
- Power BI
- DAX
- Power Query
- Data modeling
- Interactive slicers and report navigation

## Dashboard Highlights
The main dashboard displays total sales amount of $34.6M, total quantity ordered of 893K and total sales price of $30.8M in the supplied screenshot.

## Key Observations
- Modern is the leading branch in the displayed branch analysis at $13.8M.
- Zone 1 contributes $20.4M in the displayed zone analysis.
- Elizabeth Banks leads the displayed employee ranking at $5.0M.
- Augmentin leads the displayed product sales ranking at approximately $908.87K.

## Skills Demonstrated
KPI development, DAX, Power Query, interactive reporting, comparative analysis and dashboard storytelling.
