# Road Accident Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze road accident casualties and explore how casualty patterns vary by day, vehicle type, light condition, road condition, road type and living area.

## Objective
- Monitor headline accident KPIs.
- Identify patterns in casualties across time and road-related factors.
- Compare casualty distribution by vehicle type and environmental conditions.
- Provide an interactive view that supports exploratory analysis.

## Tools
- Power BI
- Data visualization
- Interactive filtering
- KPI design

## Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard displays 418K casualties, 563K vehicles and a total speed-limit measure of 12M in the supplied screenshot. It also includes breakdowns by day of week, vehicle type, light conditions, road conditions, road type and living area.

## Key Observations
- Friday has the highest displayed daily casualty count.
- Cars account for the largest displayed vehicle-type category.
- Daylight represents 72.98% of casualties in the displayed light-condition analysis.
- Dry road conditions dominate the displayed road-condition breakdown.

## Skills Demonstrated
Data visualization, KPI development, categorical analysis, interactive dashboard design and business-oriented storytelling.
