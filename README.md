# Akanimo Eyoma — Data Analytics & Business Intelligence Portfolio

This repository contains my personal data analytics and business intelligence portfolio. I created it to bring together some of the projects I have worked on while developing my skills in Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python and machine learning.

The portfolio focuses mainly on business intelligence and data analysis, with a smaller section dedicated to applied machine learning projects. Most of the projects started with a dataset and the goal of understanding what the data was saying. I then used dashboards, visualisations, calculations and analysis to present the results in a way that is easier to understand.

## Projects

### Road Accident Dashboard

This Power BI project looks at road accident data and presents the information through an interactive dashboard. The analysis covers casualties, vehicles, accident severity, road conditions, road type, light conditions and living areas. The dashboard was designed to make it easier to compare accident patterns and identify the areas with higher numbers of casualties.

### Sales Report Analysis

This Power BI project focuses on sales performance across different parts of a business. The report looks at sales by branch, zone, employee and product and uses interactive filters to allow the user to explore the results. Power Query was used for data preparation and DAX was used for calculations and measures.

### Product Sales Analysis

This Tableau project analyses product sales and profitability. It includes revenue, cost and profit KPIs as well as trends over time, product categories and geographic performance. The dashboard was built to give a general view of how different products and locations contribute to sales and profit.

### Office Supply Analysis

This Tableau project examines the performance of an office supply business. The analysis covers revenue, cost, units sold, product performance, payment channels, customer segments and monthly trends. The dashboard brings these areas together so that sales performance can be viewed from different perspectives.

### Vrinda Store Sales Analysis

This Tableau project focuses on e-commerce sales data from Vrinda Store. It looks at orders, sales channels, customer demographics, geography, order status and the products and states generating the highest sales. The aim was to understand customer and sales patterns from the available data.

### Food & Beverage Sales Analysis

This Tableau project analyses sales in a food and beverage business. The dashboard looks at revenue, orders, product categories, product groups, sales channels and salesperson performance. It provides a way to compare sales across the different parts of the business and identify areas performing better or worse.

### Nigeria Crime Risk Classification

This is one of my machine learning projects. I used K-Means clustering to group crime patterns across Nigerian states based on crime statistics. The resulting clusters were interpreted as different levels of crime risk and the model was incorporated into a FastAPI application.

The project gave me experience working with data preparation, feature scaling, K-Means clustering and model interpretation, as well as taking a machine learning model beyond a notebook and connecting it to an application.

### College Placement Prediction

This project uses the K-Nearest Neighbors algorithm to predict student placement outcomes. I built the model in Python and used saved model and categorical-encoding artifacts in a Streamlit application.

The project was useful for practising the process of taking a trained machine learning model and turning it into an interactive application where users can enter information and receive a prediction.

## Tools and Technologies

The projects in this portfolio use a combination of Excel, Power BI, Tableau, Python, Pandas, NumPy and Scikit-learn. Depending on the project, I have also worked with Power Query, DAX, K-Means, KNN, FastAPI, Streamlit and Joblib.
